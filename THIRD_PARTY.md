## Polestar guides

- Source: Polestar 0.4.1.1 (https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar), guide packs
  `Polestar_Guides`, `Polestar_Guides_Horde` and `Polestar_Guides_Alliance`.
- Copyright (c) 2026 The Polestar authors. Licence: GPL-3.0-only.
- Used in: `Guides/Polestar/*.lua`, generated by `tools/import_polestar.lua`, and read by
  `Guides/PolestarAdapter.lua`.
- Changes: the guide tables are re-serialized for AzerothRoute and run by AzerothRoute's own guide
  engine. Spawn-spot lists (`spotsPacked`), walking crumbs (`crumbs`) and smart-target candidates
  (`candidates`) are removed. Polestar's baked walking legs (`Legs.lua`, made with Leffe's
  navigation maps under a permission given to Polestar's author) and Polestar's quest, item and
  codex databases are not included.
- The quest, NPC and coordinate facts inside those guides come from the sources Polestar lists in
  `Guides/Polestar/LICENSE-DATA.txt` (Polestar's own notice, kept unchanged), among them EG Link
  output for World of Warcraft: Forever (data from Efficient Games, https://efficient.games,
  CC BY 4.0), the Forever TrinityCore world database (GPL-2.0), CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0),
  pfQuest (MIT) and Blizzard client tables. AzerothRoute would remove any of them on a rights
  holder's request.
