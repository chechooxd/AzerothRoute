# Third-party components and data

AzerothRoute is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 only (see LICENSE).
The components below keep their own licences and notices; each `addon/` folder carries a
`NOTICE.txt` saying it was modified from Polestar, and keeps Polestar's `LICENSE`,
`LICENSE-DATA.txt` and `CREDITS.md` files unchanged.

## Polestar

- Source: Polestar 0.4.1.1, https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar.
- Copyright (c) 2026 The Polestar authors. Licence: GPL-3.0-only.
- Used in: every folder of `addon/`, generated by `tools/fork_polestar.lua` (renamed, with the changes
  in `tools/fork/patches.lua`).
- Polestar's own third-party notices travel with it: `addon/AzerothRoute/LICENSE` (Shortest Path
  Forever and CMaNGOS portions), `addon/AzerothRoute_Nav/LICENSE` and `CREDITS.md`, the fonts' OFL
  texts in `addon/AzerothRoute/Media/Fonts/`, each language folder's `CREDITS.md` and
  `LICENSE-DATA.txt`, and the guide and item data notices (`LICENSE-DATA.txt`).

## Shortest Path Forever

- By Cillian Berragan, https://github.com/cjber/shortest-path-forever. Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later.
- Used in: the walking router and route lines Polestar adapted (`addon/AzerothRoute_Nav/`, and the
  files `addon/AzerothRoute/LICENSE` names).

## Icy Veins

- Talent plans: the level-by-level leveling talent builds and talent trees of Icy Veins' WoW Forever
  guides (https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/leveling-guides), credited and linked per spec in the
  plan's Source line. Only the talent choices and tree facts are used, no guide text.
  Kept in `tools/fork/icyveins/` and generated into `addon/AzerothRoute/Talents/IcyVeins.lua`.

## Data

The guides, names and item data carry facts from the sources each `LICENSE-DATA.txt` lists: EG Link
output for World of Warcraft: Forever (data from Efficient Games, https://efficient.games, CC BY 4.0),
the Forever TrinityCore world database (GPL-2.0), CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0), pfQuest (MIT),
QuestieDB facts (in the guide, QuestDB, Codex and Experimental packs, as Polestar ships them; its
maintainers ask that QuestieDB be treated as all rights reserved), and Blizzard client tables.
AzerothRoute would remove any of them on a rights holder's request.

## Left out

- Polestar_Arcade (Freedoom Arcade by petllama).
- Polestar_Guides_<Race> folders: only `Legs.lua`, baked with Leffe's navigation maps under a
  permission given to Polestar's author.
- Polestar Recorder (a separate addon).
